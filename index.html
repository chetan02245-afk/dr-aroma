The 1960 Chronicle

The Soul of Tripolia Bazar

Over Six Decades of Pure Hydro-Distillation & Bespoke Jaipur Perfumery

The Inception in 1960

In 1960, amidst the vibrant brassware artisans and historic havelis of Tripolia Bazar, D R Aroma opened its wooden carved doors. While commercial industrialization swept the globe with synthetic aroma chemicals and mass-bottled sprays, our founding master perfumer made an enduring pledge: never compromise on the living soul of pure botanicals.

The Time-Honored Art of Slow Distillation

Attar making is not industrial chemistry; it is a patient, meditative craft passed down across generations. Our workshop embraces the enduring wisdom of traditional Indian perfumery:

  • The Artisanal Stills: Handcrafted vessels filled with pure water and seasonal botanicals — whether springtime Damask roses, fragrant jasmine, or sun-baked earthen clay.
  • Gentle Steam Distillation: The vessels are sealed securely according to ancient techniques. Gentle, slow heat releases the delicate aromatic vapors without burning or altering the fragile natural fragrance molecules.
  • Traditional Sandalwood Foundation: The aromatic essence condenses and gently infuses into an aged, traditional sandalwood oil base. Over multiple careful distillations, the base absorbs the full soul of the botanicals, yielding a rich, alcohol-free attar that endures on the skin.

"A true attar does not shout from across the room. It lives on your pulse, awakening when you greet an old friend, whispering when the desert wind rises."

— Master Perfumer, D R Aroma (1960)

Why We Remain Made-to-Order

Many have urged us to mass-package fixed bottles and sell through department store chains. We have consistently declined. Every human body carries a unique lipid mantle and skin warmth. By compounding our fragrances to order, we ensure that the perfume you wear belongs entirely to you.
